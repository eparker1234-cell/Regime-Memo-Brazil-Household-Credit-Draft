# DI leg addendum (Lula scenario) v0.1
Written 25 Oct 2026, ~23:58 UK, during the runoff count: ~75% counted, Lula leading by ~2m votes,
result not yet officially called. Written BEFORE the first post-result DI session (Mon 26 Oct).
Status: hypothesis, not adopted. Entry requires own probabilities, EV and sizing (not yet built).

Context: the original institutional leg (v0.1 PDF, section 8) was a 1y–5y steepener conditional
on a Flávio win. If Lula wins, that leg is off under its own rule.

Rejected for a Lula win: long-end steepener on the 2022 template. Election-driven long-end
repricing will be consensus by Monday and is not a mispricing.

Candidate institutional leg: receive front-end DI (~F28).
Thesis: after a Lula win, the market prices fewer Selic cuts on fiscal fear (2024 precedent).
Rising household delinquency (same signal as the Nu leg: 15–90 / 90+ NPL YoY) weakens consumption
and disinflation, giving the BCB more room to keep cutting than priced.

Go / no-go test (after Monday's repricing):
- GO only if the curve prices a material easing pause vs pre-election (compare front-end implied
  cuts to Focus and to the 23 Oct close: 1y 12.96%).
- NO-GO if the curve still prices continued easing: no gap; kill the institutional leg and record why.

Main risk: Lula fiscal expansion keeps inflation sticky; BCB holds despite credit weakness
(2024 outcome); front end sells off.

Validation signals: Nu Q3'26 (~12 Nov) and BCB household NPL series; Copom 3–4 Nov language;
Focus Selic path.
